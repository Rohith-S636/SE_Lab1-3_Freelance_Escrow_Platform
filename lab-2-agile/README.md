## Sprint-report timing qualification

All three sprints were closed during the compressed Jira exercise on 12 November 2026. Sprint 1's stories appear as completed issues. Sprint 2 and Sprint 3 stories appear under **Issues completed outside this sprint** because their completion timestamps precede their planned start dates. The recorded history is preserved and the discrepancy is documented in the report.

## Interpretation

This is an academic Jira workflow simulation, not a software delivery report. The Done statuses do not prove that the platform features were implemented, tested, or deployed.
