## Sprint-report timing qualification

The three sprints were closed during the compressed Jira exercise on 12 November 2026. Sprint 1's stories appear as completed issues. Sprint 2 and Sprint 3 stories appear in Jira's **Issues completed outside this sprint** section because their completion timestamps precede their planned start dates.

Planned date windows retained in Jira:
- Sprint 1: 12–19 November 2026
- Sprint 2: 19–26 November 2026
- Sprint 3: 26 November–3 December 2026

The discrepancy is intentionally disclosed. Do not alter the recorded history or describe the planned date windows as actual execution dates.

## Important interpretation

This is an **academic Jira workflow simulation**, not a software delivery report. The Done statuses do not prove that the escrow platform features were implemented, tested, or deployed. Feature completion requires separate implementation and acceptance-test evidence.
